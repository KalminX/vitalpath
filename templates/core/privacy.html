Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase educational materials or join our waitlist, we collect standard transaction information including your name, email address, and order history. We do not store payment card details directly; all payments are processed securely by Stripe.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Deliver purchased educational guides and course curriculum access
  • Send order receipts and important updates regarding course materials
  • Respond to customer inquiries and support requests
  • Send waitlist and announcement notifications with your explicit consent

3. Third-Party Services

We partner with trusted service providers including Stripe (payment infrastructure) and secure email delivery services. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketers.

4. Contact & Data Requests

To request data deletion or review your stored records, please reach out to privacy@vitalpath.edu.
